Dear Shereen,

In this neon midnight terminal,
you are the soft pink light between keystrokes,
the secret line of poetry hidden inside the code,
the one signal my heart always finds first.

When the world turns noisy,
your love is my quiet syntax,
steady, elegant, true.
You have met me with patience,
held me through every crash and reboot,
and made ordinary days bloom like roses in winter.

You turn fear into courage,
turn doubt into laughter,
and every "almost" into "we can."
With you, even small moments feel golden,
and every day feels like something worth celebrating.

I love you more than words can hold,
and I am so, so happy that I get to marry you.
I cannot wait to build our joyful life together:
late-night tea, inside jokes,
slow mornings, loud dancing,
and a home filled with warmth, grace, and love.

Happy Valentine's Day, my heart.

Forever yours,
Jay
